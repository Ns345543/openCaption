openCaption — third-party notices
================================

This extension ships one third-party program and one small compatibility script.

-------------------

without installing anything.

                 https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/#sources

json2.js
--------
Bundled as jsx/json2.js so ExtendScript has JSON.stringify/JSON.parse on
Premiere builds whose ES3 engine does not provide them.

  Project      : https://github.com/douglascrockford/JSON-js
  License      : Public Domain (dedicated by Douglas Crockford)
  Version      : 2023-05-10

json2.js is included unmodified apart from a short header comment.
